# Demo store images

`pnpm seed` uses `images/<name>.webp`, `.jpg` or `.png` for each name below when the file
is there, and a plain shot in the product's colour when it isn't. Each image may be up to
20 MB; 1600 × 2000 px (4:5, portrait) WebP or JPEG at about 85% quality is plenty.

The store is **Mycelium Works**, a mycology lab and farm (nowhere in particular). The images should look
like one brand shot them in one studio.

## Style (prepend to every prompt)

> Professional e-commerce product photograph, 4:5 portrait, shot in a calm studio with
> soft daylight from the upper left and gentle shadows. Background: warm off-white
> seamless paper (#FAFAF9), with a single muted lime-green accent somewhere in the scene
> (a card, a cloth, a tape, a sprig) in #B8E948 or #D9F7A5. Natural textures, matte
> surfaces, no clutter, no text, no logos, no people's faces. The product fills about 60%
> of the frame, centred, slightly above the middle, with room around it for cropping.
> Photorealistic, sharp focus, true colours.

Category images (the four `category-*`) are wider scenes: same light and palette, a
lifestyle composition rather than a single product, 4:5 too.
