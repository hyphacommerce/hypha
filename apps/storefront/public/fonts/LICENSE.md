# Fonts

Fraunces (The Fraunces Project Authors, github.com/undercasetype/Fraunces) and Instrument
Sans (Rodrigo Fuenzalida, github.com/Instrument/instrument-sans), both under the SIL Open
Font License 1.1 (scripts.sil.org/OFL). The files are the variable, subsetted builds of
the `@fontsource-variable/fraunces` and `@fontsource-variable/instrument-sans` 5.3.0
packages; `app/globals.css` declares them.
