# Code of Conduct

Hypha follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep disagreements about
the work, not the person. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks aren't tolerated
in any project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat).

Report unacceptable behaviour to conduct@hyphacommerce.com. Reports are handled in
confidence by the maintainers, who may warn, restrict or ban anyone who breaks these rules.
